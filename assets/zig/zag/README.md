# Zag

A zig is like a bumble bee buzzing around in a particular direction. To zag means to go in a separate direction to it's original zig. The bee neither zigs nor zags, but it's entire motion is a zigzag pattern. This is basically a culmination of my zig projects. Although these projects may zag, I will try and conform them to the zig. Although the zig project may zig or zag occassionally too, I will try and conform my zag to their zigzag patterns, at least until 1.0 hits. Then I will settle on a certain zig or zag.
